Dot phrases

Free note templates for family medicine: OB, inpatient, clinic, well child, patient instructions, problem blocks, exams and more. Copy one into your EHR's auto text or SmartPhrase editor.

How to read these
  • Cerner view: _ is a blank to fill in. _a / b / c means pick one. [VITALS] and other bracketed names are Cerner smart templates; swap in your facility's equivalent.
  • Epic view: *** is a blank (F2 jumps to the next one). {a/b/c} is a pick-list: Epic can't create a dropdown from pasted text, so build it as a SmartList in the SmartPhrase editor. @VITALS@-style names stand for SmartLinks; names vary by organization, so swap in yours. Bracketed names left over are Cerner templates with no generic Epic equivalent.
  • local after a blank marks an institution-specific detail (protocols, phone numbers, cutoffs). Fill in your own.
  • Templates, not medical advice. Check doses and recommendations against current guidance before use, and never paste patient information into anything public.

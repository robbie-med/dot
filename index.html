Dot phrases

About 950 note templates for family medicine: OB and newborn, hospital, clinic, problem-based assessment and plan, exams, patient handouts and reference sheets. Copy one into Cerner Auto Text or an Epic SmartPhrase and make it yours.

How they're organized

The first character of every abbreviation says where it's used. Type it in your EHR and the matching phrases come up together.

%
OB, L&D and newborn. Admission H&P to discharge, prenatal visits, newborn care.
&
Inpatient. Admissions, progress notes, handoffs, discharges.
$
Clinic. Visit notes, well child checks, handouts, result letters.
#
One problem. An assessment-and-plan block; stack several under any note.
.
Anywhere. Exams, small fragments, consult prep, attestations, reference sheets.

The letters after the symbol name the kind of phrase: pi patient instructions, lr lab result sentences, h HPI, wcc well child, wu workup, c consult prep, pe/e exam, ed physician reference (not for the chart). So $pi is a clinic handout, %pi a pregnancy one, and &h an admission HPI.

Reading a phrase

Cerner format: _ is a blank. _yes / no means pick one. Bracketed names like [VITALS] are Cerner smart templates; swap in your facility's.

Epic format: *** is a blank (F2 jumps to the next). {yes/no} marks a pick-list: Epic can't build a dropdown from pasted text, so make it a SmartList in the SmartPhrase editor. @VITALS@ and the like stand for SmartLinks, whose names vary by organization. A bracketed name left over is a Cerner template with no general Epic match.

local after a blank marks a detail that differs by institution: a protocol, a cutoff, a phone number.
